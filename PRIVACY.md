# AllIn privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

AllIn is a messaging app built on the open-source Signal app. It is made by Jason, for family and friends, and is
not affiliated with Signal Messenger LLC, Google or ESPN. This policy explains what AllIn does with your data.

**In short: AllIn has no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads, and it never sells or shares your data.**

## Signal messages and calls

AllIn connects to Signal's servers, exactly like the Signal app. Messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, and
Signal's own privacy policy applies to its servers: https://signal.org/legal/. Your messages are stored only on
your devices.

## Contacts

With your permission, AllIn reads your phone's contacts on the device to show names, and uses Signal's private
contact discovery to find which contacts use Signal (as the Signal app does). Contacts are not uploaded anywhere else.

## Google Voice (optional, off by default)

If you turn it on and sign in, AllIn reads and sends your Google Voice texts through Google's Voice website on your
behalf. Your Google sign-in stays on your device. Google Voice texts are fetched from Google when needed, kept in
memory only, and never sent anywhere except to Google. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Fantasy Football (optional, off by default)

If you turn it on and sign in to ESPN, AllIn reads your fantasy league from ESPN. Your ESPN sign-in stays on your
device. ESPN's privacy policy applies.

## Location and Find My Device (optional, off by default)

Only if you turn on Settings › Family, AllIn collects this phone's location, including when the app is closed or
not in use, and its battery level. It sends them, end-to-end encrypted over Signal, only to the family's bot account
shown on that screen, so your family can see where the phone is and ring it if it's lost. A notification shows
while location is shared. Turn it off on the same screen at any time; the family's server keeps the last
positions it received.

## Photos, camera, microphone and files

Used only when you send or take a photo, make a call, or record a voice message, as in Signal.

## Children

AllIn is meant for family members, including children whose parents set it up. It collects no data for any other
purpose than those above.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling AllIn deletes everything it stores on the device. To delete your Signal account, use Settings ›
Account › Delete account. To stop location sharing, turn off Settings › Family.
